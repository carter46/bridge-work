Your Data

Privacy Notice

This notice explains what Hubjob Platform collects when you apply through this website, why we collect it, how long we keep it and how to ask us to delete it.

Who we are

Hubjob Platform is the data controller for the information you submit on this website. You can reach us at info@hubjobplatform.com, by phone on +44 1202 958648, or by post at 42 Windham Road, Bournemouth, Dorset, BH1 2AW, UK.

What we collect

When you submit the application or contact form we store:

  • Your full name, email address and phone number.
  • Your country of residence, your preferred work type and, if you choose to tell us, whether you have the right to work in the UK or EU.
  • Your confirmation that you are 18 or over, and the time you agreed to this notice.
  • The role or job you applied for and any CV you upload.
  • Technical details used to stop spam and abuse: a one-way hash of your IP address (we do not store the address itself) and the page and campaign link you arrived from.

We do not ask for your age, date of birth or nationality, and you should not include sensitive information (such as health, religion or ethnicity) in your CV.

Why we use it

  • To review your application and contact you about the role you applied for or similar roles that match your profile.
  • To share your details with an employer only when we put you forward for one of their roles.
  • To send you an email confirming that we received your application.
  • To protect the website and our team from spam and misuse.

We rely on your consent, given when you tick the box on the form, and on our legitimate interest in running a secure recruitment service. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

How we protect your CV

CVs are stored outside the public website and can only be opened by authorised members of our recruitment team after signing in. Every time a CV is viewed or downloaded the access is recorded. Uploaded files are checked for type and size before they are accepted.

How long we keep it

We keep your application and CV for 12 months after it was last updated. After that the CV file is deleted and your personal details are permanently removed from our records. If you are in an active hiring process with us we may keep your application for longer, and we will remove it as soon as that process ends. Spam-protection records are deleted after 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Send you a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your application and CV at any time, without waiting for the retention period to end.
  • Stop contacting you about new roles.

Email info@hubjobplatform.com from the address you applied with and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses Google's advertising tag to measure how visitors find us. The application form itself does not set cookies. If a security check (such as Cloudflare Turnstile or Google reCAPTCHA) is shown on the form, that provider may process technical data to tell people and bots apart.

Questions about your data?

Our team replies within 24 business hours.
