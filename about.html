Hubjob Platform is a recruitment service based in the United Kingdom. We match job seekers with employers in the UK, Europe and remote teams worldwide, and we never charge candidates a fee.

Who we are

We are recruiters who work with job seekers from many countries. Some want their first role abroad, some want flexible remote work, and some simply want a better-paid job. Our work is the same in every case: understand what the person can do, and introduce them to an employer who needs exactly that.

Every role we list shows its pay. When an employer has passed our legal, pay and compliance checks, their listings are marked “Verified employer” so you know who you are dealing with.

How we work

No fees for candidates

You will never pay us to apply, to be interviewed or to be placed. There are no hidden charges.

Clear pay and terms

Each listing states its pay range and working arrangement up front, so you can decide before you apply.

Support beyond the offer

For roles abroad we guide you through visas, paperwork and your first weeks with the new employer.

Our mission

To connect international talent with trusted employers through fair, transparent recruitment, and to support each person as their career grows.

Our vision

A job market where skill, not nationality, decides who gets hired, and every move abroad is safe, legal and fairly paid.
