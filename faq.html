Frequently asked questions

Answers about applying, pay, eligibility and how we look after your data. Can't find what you need? Contact us.

search

How do I apply for a job?add

Find the role on our jobs page and click Apply Now. The application form opens with that role already selected. Fill in your details, attach your CV if you have one, and send it.

Can I send my CV without applying for a specific job?add

Yes. Use Upload CV and leave the role empty or describe the kind of work you want. We will contact you when a suitable role opens.

Do I need a CV to apply?add

No. A CV helps us match you faster, but you can apply with just your contact details and we will ask for more information if we need it.

Which CV file types can I upload?add

PDF, Word (.doc or .docx), RTF or plain text (.txt), up to 2 MB.

How long until I hear back?add

We review every application and reply within 24 business hours, by email or on the WhatsApp number you give us.

Can I apply for more than one role?add

Yes. Apply to each role separately so we know which ones interest you.

Do I have to pay anything?add

No. Applying, interviews and placement are free for candidates. If anyone asks you for money in our name, do not pay and tell us.

How is pay shown on listings?add

Each listing shows its pay range and period, for example $35–$90 per hour. The hourly pay filter on the jobs page uses US dollars, and yearly or monthly pay is converted to an hourly figure for that filter.

Are the jobs remote?add

Many are. Every listing says whether it is remote, hybrid or on-site, and which countries applicants can apply from. You can filter by both on the jobs page.

What does “Verified employer” mean?add

The employer has passed four checks: we confirmed the company is legally registered, reviewed that the pay is fair, confirmed the contract and any visa sponsorship are genuine, and the employer agreed never to charge candidates.

Which industries do you recruit for?add

Technology, customer support, administration and operations, marketing, video, sales, design and education. The categories on our homepage show how many roles are open in each.

I'm an employer. How do I list a role?add

Email info@hubjobplatform.com with the role, location and pay. We will reply with next steps and send you suitable candidates.

Who can apply?add

Anyone aged 18 or over. Some roles are only open to applicants in certain countries or regions, and this is shown on each listing.

Do I need the right to work in the UK or EU?add

Not for roles open worldwide. For roles limited to the UK or Europe, the listing says so. The application form asks about your right to work, but answering is optional.

Do you help with visas?add

For roles that involve moving abroad, we guide you through the visa process and paperwork with the employer, and support you through your first weeks in the job.

Is my personal information safe?add

Yes. CVs are stored outside the public website and only our recruitment team can open them after signing in. We share your details only with employers relevant to the roles you apply for, in line with UK GDPR.

How long do you keep my application?add

12 months after it was last updated. After that your CV and personal details are permanently deleted.

How do I ask you to delete my data?add

Email info@hubjobplatform.com from the address you applied with. We respond within one month. Our privacy notice has the full details.
